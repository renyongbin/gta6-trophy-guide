Road to the platinum — about 200 hours

Built from the official trophy descriptions: story first, then the 100% checklist, then cleanup. There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start of each chapter. Cheats disable trophies and saving (PowerPyx).

    ⚠ The one trophy that may be missable

    Positive Mental Attitude (bronze) — meditate at every spot across Leonida. In Chapter 1, the morning after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen at Jason's house, follow her to the balcony and choose Accept. That session is gone after Chapter 1, and it is still unconfirmed whether it counts toward the trophy — do it to be safe. Every other trophy can be earned in free-roam after the story. Source: PowerPyx, All Meditation Spot Locations.
